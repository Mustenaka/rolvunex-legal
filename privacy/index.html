Rolvunex 隐私政策

版本：V3　生效日期：2026 年 9 月 16 日　更新日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

重点提示：Rolvunex 无需注册账号，不联网，也不集成任何广告、统计或推送类 SDK。我们只在你的手机本地处理连接和控制卷闸盖所必需的信息，不会把任何个人信息上传到我们或第三方的服务器。请在使用前仔细阅读本政策，特别是以加粗方式标示的内容。

一、我们处理哪些信息

本应用的基本功能是通过蓝牙搜索、连接和控制你的卷闸盖。为实现这一功能，本应用会在你的手机本地处理下列信息。除此之外，本应用不会收集你的姓名、手机号码、通讯录、照片、位置、设备 IMEI、广告标识符等其他信息。

（一）搜索设备

当你点击「搜索」时，本应用会扫描附近蓝牙广播，按 iRoller 设备名称筛选卷闸盖，并在列表中显示：

  • 设备的蓝牙名称、蓝牙地址（MAC 地址）和信号强度。

扫描可能收到其他蓝牙设备的广播，本应用会丢弃不符合名称规则的结果。搜索结果只保存在内存中，应用进程结束后清除。

「蓝牙自动感应」开启时，本应用会在首次成功连接后记住设备，使用按该地址过滤的扫描寻找它；后台和锁屏时也可继续等待、回连及收发状态。新安装默认开启此开关，你可以在设备设置中关闭；主动断开或解除本机绑定也会关闭自动感应。系统 HID 输入设备配对和连接由 Android 管理。

（二）连接与控制设备

为了连接并控制你选择的设备，本应用会处理：

  • 设备蓝牙地址：保存在本机，用于下次连接同一台设备。
  • 绑定 ID 与配对 PIN：绑定 ID 是本应用发给设备的每条指令都要携带的 3 字节识别码，出厂默认为 66 66 66，本应用不提供修改入口；配对 PIN 在系统蓝牙配对时使用，出厂由绑定 ID 换算得出。你在本应用中修改配对 PIN 并经设备确认成功后，新 PIN 会保存在本机，用于在「配对 PIN」页面显示。二者相当于设备的访问凭据，请妥善保管。
  • 设备状态与设备信息：设备上报的开合位置、运行方向、照明、负载电流档位、蓝牙感应距离、故障代码、行程标定状态与行程脉冲数、车辆行驶 / 静止状态、固件版本、硬件编码和型号，用于在界面上显示设备的当前状态。当前状态只保存在内存中，应用进程结束后清除。每次连接会重新查询，断线期间不把旧位置显示为当前状态。
  • 车辆行驶状态：由设备内的加速度传感器（G-sensor）判定后通过蓝牙上报，只有「行驶中」「静止」等状态，不含位置、速度或行驶轨迹。本应用仅用它判断是否发送行车未关盖提醒，不保存、不上传。
  • 行程标定：标定时记录的全关、全开端点保存在设备中，本应用不保存标定数据，只在本机记录已提示过标定的设备，避免重复提示。
  • 自动回连信号门限：本机单独保存上次设备确认的感应距离档位，仅用于选择回连扫描的信号强度门限，不用来推断手机位置，也不代替设备的实时状态。保存到解除本机绑定、撤回同意或卸载为止。
  • 控制指令：你发出的开启、关闭、暂停、调整开合位置、照明、档位等指令，通过蓝牙直接发送到设备，不经过任何服务器。

（三）蓝牙调试记录

「蓝牙调试」页面会记录最近 300 条收发报文，包括时间、报文内容以及设备名称或地址，用于排查连接问题。记录只保存在内存中，应用进程结束或点击「清空」即删除。只有当你点击「复制全部」时，记录才会被写入系统剪贴板。

（四）偏好设置

本应用会在本机保存你选择的语言、界面外观、是否已完成首次设置、蓝牙自动感应开关、行车未关盖提醒开关，以及你同意本政策时的版本号，以便下次打开时沿用。

（五）剪贴板

当你点击「复制」时，配对 PIN 或蓝牙调试记录会被写入系统剪贴板，方便你粘贴到其他地方。本应用不会读取你的剪贴板。

二、我们申请的系统权限

本应用只在你使用相应功能时才申请权限，并在申请时说明用途。你可以拒绝授权，拒绝后只是无法搜索和连接设备，其他页面仍可正常浏览。

  • 附近的设备（Android 12 及以上）：包括 BLUETOOTH_SCAN 和 BLUETOOTH_CONNECT 两项权限，用于搜索、连接卷闸盖并收发控制指令。申请时机：你第一次搜索或连接设备时。本应用已向系统声明不会利用蓝牙扫描推断你的位置。
  • 位置信息（仅 Android 11 及以下）：ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION 权限。这些版本的 Android 系统要求应用获得位置权限并打开系统定位服务，才能扫描到蓝牙设备。本应用不调用任何定位接口，不获取、不保存你的位置。申请时机：你第一次搜索设备时。
  • 蓝牙（仅 Android 11 及以下）：BLUETOOTH 和 BLUETOOTH_ADMIN 两项权限，属于系统在安装时自动授予的普通权限，用于使用蓝牙功能，不会弹窗申请。
  • 连接设备前台服务与保持唤醒：FOREGROUND_SERVICE、FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE 和 WAKE_LOCK 是系统自动授予的普通权限，用于自动感应开启时保持后台连接服务，并在连接期间维持设备要求的心跳。服务会提供系统运行提示；关闭自动感应、解除本机绑定或撤回同意后停止。保持连接会增加耗电。
  • 通知（Android 13 及以上）：POST_NOTIFICATIONS 权限，用于在设备报告车辆行驶而卷闸盖未闭合时发送系统提醒。申请时机：设备第一次上报行车状态，或你在设置中打开「行车未关盖提醒」时。拒绝后不影响其他功能，只是无法显示该提醒；Android 12 及以下无需单独申请，你可以在系统通知设置中关闭。

此外，本应用使用的 AndroidX 组件会在安装包中声明一项名为 com.iroller.smart.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION 的签名级权限，仅用于保护本应用内部的广播通信，不涉及任何个人信息，也不会向你申请。

本应用不申请网络、相机、麦克风、存储、通讯录、电话、短信、日历等权限。你可以随时在手机「设置 → 应用 → Rolvunex → 权限」中查看或关闭已授予的权限（不同品牌手机的菜单名称可能略有不同）。

三、信息的保存

  1. 保存地点：需要保存的信息只存放在你手机上本应用的私有存储空间内，其他应用无法读取。我们没有服务器，不会把这些信息传输到你的手机以外，也不存在向境外提供个人信息的情形。
  2. 保存期限：设备地址保存到你解除本机绑定、撤回同意或卸载本应用为止；绑定 ID、经设备确认的配对 PIN、蓝牙自动感应开关、行车未关盖提醒开关、已提示过行程标定的设备、首次设置状态和同意记录保存到你撤回同意或卸载本应用为止；语言和界面外观保存到你卸载本应用为止；搜索结果、设备状态和蓝牙调试记录只在本次运行期间保存在内存中。卸载本应用后，系统会删除本应用保存的全部数据。
  3. 系统备份：如果你开启了手机系统的备份或换机迁移功能，上述本机设置可能随系统备份保存到你的系统账户云空间，或迁移到你的新手机。该过程由手机操作系统及其服务提供方完成，我们无法访问这些备份。

四、信息的共享、转让与公开披露

由于本应用不会把你的个人信息传输到手机以外，我们不会共享、转让或公开披露你的个人信息。本应用发出的控制指令（含绑定 ID）只通过蓝牙发送给你选择连接的卷闸盖。

本应用集成了下列第三方开源组件。它们随本应用安装在你的手机上，仅用于实现界面与三维画面等功能，不收集你的个人信息；本应用没有申请网络权限，这些组件也无法联网发送数据：

  • Android Jetpack（AndroidX）与 Jetpack Compose：提供方 Google LLC，用于界面显示与应用生命周期管理。
  • Filament：提供方 Google LLC，用于渲染车辆与卷闸盖的三维模型。
  • Kotlin 标准库与 kotlinx.coroutines：提供方 JetBrains s.r.o.，用于程序运行与异步任务。

五、信息的保护

  1. 需要保存的信息只存放在本应用的私有存储空间内；本应用不联网，避免了信息在网络传输或服务器存储过程中泄露的风险。
  2. 设备只处理携带正确绑定 ID 的指令，系统蓝牙配对需要输入配对 PIN。
  3. 请为手机设置锁屏密码，不要把绑定 ID、配对 PIN 或蓝牙调试记录透露给无关人员；转让车辆或设备、更换手机前，请先解除本机绑定。
  4. 如果发生个人信息安全事件，我们将按照法律法规的要求，及时告知你事件的基本情况和可能的影响、我们已经或将要采取的处置措施、你可以自主防范和降低风险的建议等，并依法向有关主管部门报告。

六、你的权利

你可以通过以下方式在本应用内管理你的信息：

  1. 查阅：在「设置」中查看已连接的设备、蓝牙感应距离等信息；在「设置 → 配对 PIN 与绑定 ID」中查看绑定 ID 和配对 PIN；在「设置 → 蓝牙调试」中查看收发记录。
  2. 更正：在「设置 → 配对 PIN 与绑定 ID」中修改绑定 ID。
  3. 删除：在「设置 → 蓝牙设备」中点击「解除本机绑定」，删除保存的设备地址；在「蓝牙调试」中点击「清空」，删除收发记录；在「关于」中撤回同意，删除本机保存的设备信息；卸载本应用，删除全部数据。
  4. 撤回同意：你可以在系统设置中关闭已授予的权限；也可以在「关于」中点击「撤回同意并清除本机数据」，本应用会断开设备，清除本机保存的设备地址、绑定 ID 和自动感应设置，并在你重新同意之前不再处理你的信息。撤回同意不影响撤回前基于你的同意已经进行的处理。
  5. 注销账号：本应用没有账号体系，不涉及账号注销。卸载本应用即可删除本应用在手机上保存的全部信息。
  6. 获取副本：在「蓝牙调试」中点击「复制全部」，获取收发记录的副本。
  7. 自动化决策：本应用不会基于你的信息进行自动化决策、个性化推荐或定向推送。

如果你在行使上述权利时遇到问题，可以通过本政策第九部分的方式联系我们，我们将在 15 个工作日内答复。

1. What information we handle

The app's basic function is to find, connect to and control your roller cover over Bluetooth. To do that, it handles the information below locally on your phone. Apart from this, the app does not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, location, IMEI, advertising identifier or any other information.

(1) Searching for devices

When you tap Search, the app scans nearby Bluetooth advertisements, selects covers by their iRoller device names and lists:

  • Each device's Bluetooth name, Bluetooth (MAC) address and signal strength.

The scan may receive advertisements from other Bluetooth devices; the app discards results that do not match its name rules. Search results are kept in memory only and cleared when the app process ends.

With Auto proximity enabled, the app remembers the device after a successful connection and uses an address-filtered scan to find it again. It can keep waiting, reconnecting and exchanging status in the background and while the screen is locked. This switch is enabled by default on new installations and can be turned off in device settings. Explicitly disconnecting or unbinding also turns it off. Android manages system HID input-device pairing and connections.

(2) Connecting to and controlling the device

To connect to and control the device you choose, the app handles:

  • The device's Bluetooth address, stored on your phone so the app can reconnect to the same device.
  • The bind ID and pairing PIN. The bind ID is a 3-byte code carried by every command the app sends to the device. It is 66 66 66 by default and the app offers no way to change it. The pairing PIN is used when your phone pairs with the device; by default it is derived from the bind ID. If you change the pairing PIN in the app and the device confirms the change, the new PIN is stored on your phone so the Pairing PIN page can show it. Together they act as the key to your device, so keep them safe.
  • Device status and details reported by the device: cover position, motor direction, light, motor current limit, proximity range, fault code, travel calibration status and travel pulses, whether the vehicle is driving or parked, firmware version, hardware code and model. Current status is kept in memory only and cleared when the app process ends. Every connection queries it again; an old position is not shown as current while disconnected.
  • Vehicle driving status: the device determines it with its built-in accelerometer (G-sensor) and reports it over Bluetooth. It only says whether the vehicle is driving or parked and contains no location, speed or route. The app uses it only to decide whether to send an open-cover driving alert, and does not store or upload it.
  • Travel calibration: the fully closed and fully open ends recorded during calibration are stored on the device. The app does not store calibration data; it only records on your phone which devices it has already suggested calibrating, so it does not ask again.
  • Automatic reconnection signal threshold: the app separately stores the last proximity range confirmed by the device, solely to choose the signal-strength threshold for reconnection scans. It does not use this to derive your phone's location or as a substitute for live device status. It is kept until you unbind, withdraw consent or uninstall.
  • Your commands — open, close, pause, set position, light, current limit and so on — which go straight to the device over Bluetooth and never pass through any server.

(3) Bluetooth debug log

The Bluetooth debug page records the latest 300 frames sent and received, including the time, the frame content and the device name or address, to help troubleshoot connections. The log is kept in memory only and is deleted when the app process ends or you tap Clear. It is written to the system clipboard only when you tap Copy all.

(4) Preferences

The app stores on your phone your language, appearance, whether first-time setup is complete, the Auto proximity switch, the open-cover driving alert switch, and the version of this policy you agreed to, so they carry over the next time you open the app.

(5) Clipboard

When you tap Copy, the pairing PIN or the Bluetooth debug log is written to the system clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. The app never reads your clipboard.

2. System permissions we request

The app asks for a permission only when you use a feature that needs it, and explains why at that moment. You can say no; you just won't be able to search for or connect to the device, and every other screen keeps working.

  • Nearby devices (Android 12 and later): BLUETOOTH_SCAN and BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, used to find and connect to your cover and to send and receive commands. When requested: the first time you search for or connect to a device. The app declares to the system that it never uses Bluetooth scans to derive your location.
  • Location (Android 11 and earlier only): ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION. These Android versions only return Bluetooth scan results to apps that hold the location permission and have system location services turned on. The app never calls any location API and never reads or stores your location. When requested: the first time you search for a device.
  • Bluetooth (Android 11 and earlier only): BLUETOOTH and BLUETOOTH_ADMIN, normal permissions the system grants at install time so the app can use Bluetooth. You are not asked for them.
  • Connected-device foreground service and wake lock: FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE and WAKE_LOCK are normal permissions granted by the system. With Auto proximity enabled, they support the background connection service and the heartbeat required by the controller while connected. The service provides a system running indicator. Turning off Auto proximity, unbinding or withdrawing consent stops it. Maintaining the connection uses additional battery power.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later): POST_NOTIFICATIONS, used to alert you when the device reports that the vehicle is driving while the cover is not closed. When requested: the first time the device reports its driving status, or when you turn on the open-cover driving alert in Settings. Declining does not affect other features; only the alert cannot appear. Android 12 and earlier need no separate request, and you can turn notifications off in the system notification settings.

The AndroidX libraries used by the app also declare a signature-level permission named com.iroller.smart.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It only protects broadcasts inside the app, involves no personal information and is never requested from you.

The app does not request network, camera, microphone, storage, contacts, phone, SMS or calendar permissions. You can review or revoke granted permissions at any time under Settings → Apps → Rolvunex → Permissions (menu names vary between phone makers).

3. How information is stored

  1. Where: Everything the app stores stays in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. We have no servers, nothing is transferred off your phone, and no personal information is transferred outside China.
  2. How long: The device address is kept until you unbind this phone, withdraw consent or uninstall the app. The bind ID, the pairing PIN confirmed by the device, the Auto proximity switch, the open-cover driving alert switch, the list of devices already offered calibration, the setup status and your consent record are kept until you withdraw consent or uninstall the app. Language and appearance are kept until you uninstall the app. Search results, device status and the Bluetooth debug log exist in memory only while the app is running. When you uninstall the app, the system deletes all of its data.
  3. System backups: If you have turned on your phone's backup or device-transfer features, these settings may be included in a backup stored in your system account's cloud storage, or moved to your new phone. That is done by the operating system and its service providers; we cannot access those backups.
